Talko MEA8000 user manual

Talko MEA8000 brings the character of the Philips MEA8000 speech synthesizer to Talko 2. Play French and English speech, explore Emy syllables, and turn the Formant bank into a live vowel synthesizer.

Version 1.0. These instructions cover the Talko MEA8000 firmware for Talko 2 (RP2040), with fourteen banks, 258 speech selections, and a continuous Formant synth.

Play your first word

  1. Connect Talko’s audio output to your mixer or modular system.
  2. Select bank 0 with the encoder. Turn SOUND fully counterclockwise to select BONJOUR.
  3. Put MODE in the left position. Start with SPEED and PITCH near noon, and FX fully counterclockwise.
  4. Press SPEAK, or trigger GATE IN. Turn SOUND to choose another entry. SPEED and PITCH remain live while the word plays.

Use the encoder and display

The encoder stops at both ends of the bank and FX lists. At power on, Talko starts in bank 0 with FX off.

MODE switch: gate response

On bank 9, changing MODE or selecting another bank stops the active synth note or drone. Each MODE change shows a reminder for 550 ms: S/G/r for speech, or 1/G/d for synth, before returning to the bank/FX display.

Use the panel controls

Play the live Formant bank

  1. Select bank 9, put MODE in the middle, and turn FX fully counterclockwise.
  2. Put SOUND and PITCH near noon. Hold SPEAK, or maintain a gate, to hear a sustained vowel.
  3. Sweep SOUND through Oo → Oh → Ah → Eh → Ee.
  4. Turn SPEED clockwise to add automatic movement around the SOUND position. Fully left holds a steady vowel.
  5. Hold ERROR to hold the vowel as a note. Turn PITCH to play it, or turn SPEED past noon to add flutter. Release ERROR to resume normal movement. Release SPEAK or the gate to release the note.

SPEED sets a motion rate of about 0.15 to 12 cycles per second. The movement spans one vowel position either side of SOUND and reflects at the ends. SOUND itself follows a short fixed glide. Use right MODE for a latched drone and left MODE for short notes.

Use the ERROR button

While ERROR is held, the current display glyph flickers with sharp brightness dips and brief blackouts. Release it to restore normal brightness. The bank/FX decimal-point meaning is preserved.

Speech banks: press ERROR during a word to sustain that instant as a note. Turn PITCH to play the note while holding ERROR. A captured consonant keeps some hiss. Set SPEED at noon or lower for a steady note, then turn it past noon to add flutter. FX stays live. In G/r modes the hold continues after SPEAK/GATE is released. Release ERROR to resume if the gate is held, or stop if it is off.

Formant bank 9: hold ERROR to sustain the current vowel and pause its bend. Turn PITCH to tune the note, adjust FX, or turn SPEED above noon for flutter. Releasing a gated synth note still works while ERROR is held.

Sound banks

Bank E adds 20 electronics words. Banks b and n each contain two words. Bank 9 is generated continuously through the same MEA8000 engine.

Bank 9: Live Formant synth

Continuous vowel synthesis: Oo → Oh → Ah → Eh → Ee. See the Formant-bank instructions above.

Bank C — Citroën

Turn the encoder one click above 9 to reach C. Turn SOUND clockwise for MOTEUR, MARCHE, ARRÊT, COMMANDE, TRANSMISSION, AUTOMATIQUE, TENSION, PRESSION, NIVEAU, MINIMUM, MESURE, TEST, TERMINÉ, SYSTÈME, PANNE, ALARME, CODE D'ACCÈS, INCORRECT, PAROLE, SYNTHÉTISEUR. Trigger adjacent words for TRANSMISSION AUTOMATIQUE, NIVEAU MINIMUM, TEST TERMINÉ or CODE D’ACCÈS INCORRECT. Hold ERROR during a word and turn PITCH to sustain it as a note.

Bank E — Électronique

Turn the encoder one click past C. The SOUND knob selects NUMÉRIQUE, VOLUME, HAUTEUR, PUISSANCE, ÉLECTRICITÉ, MONOCHROME, CASSETTE, DISQUETTE, INTERRUPTEUR, COMPTEUR, BLOCAGE, CLAVIER, CURSEUR, JOYSTICK, ÉCRAN, LECTEUR, MAGNÉTOPHONE, ANALYSE, PROFONDEUR, LUMIÈRE, in that order. VOLUME uses the electronics recording from BLOC 14. For a phrase, trigger ÉCRAN and then MONOCHROME.

Banks b and n — Binaire and Oui / Non

Turn one click past E for b. With SOUND on the left, trigger ZÉRO. With SOUND on the right, trigger UN. One more click selects n, with OUI on the left and NON on the right. The selection changes near noon.

Bank 5 retains the historical radio alphabet from the source, including MICHAEL for M. Bank 6 combines the original extra words with number prefixes and suffixes, plus TELEPHONE NUMBER and ONE HUNDRED. These additions are assembled from MEA8000 frames. This version contains French and English material; it has no German vocabulary bank.

Effects

Tap the encoder to enter the FX page, select an effect, then turn the FX knob clockwise. The decimal point stays on while the FX page is selected. Formant, Ring and Whisper are dry fully counterclockwise. Bend is neutral around noon; turn left for down and right for up.

b is the combined down/up bend. Robot has been removed. There is no delay or reverb effect in this firmware.

To hear the full bend, turn FX away from noon and trigger a fresh word or note. A sustained synth note settles at its final bend pitch. Return FX to noon for the natural voice.

PITCH remains live. In bank 9, SPEED also influences the Bend attack time; with automatic vowel motion stopped, the bend takes about 250 ms. Effects can be changed during a note.

Read labels over USB serial

Connect a USB data cable and open Talko’s serial port at 115200 baud. The device identifies itself as Talko 2 MEA8000 1.0. Talko sends the spoken label on its own line when the word starts. The last word stays visible in Console’s Words display after playback ends. Each repeat sends the label again. Mode and debug data are not printed normally; diagnostics require an explicit request or debug 1.

The firmware provides serial diagnostics. It does not provide MIDI control or the playback-aligned phoneme mouth animation used by the S.A.M. firmware. Close a serial monitor before another application connects to the same port.

Install or switch firmware

Download Talko_MEA8000-1.0.uf2 for Talko 2 (RP2040) and keep a copy of the standard firmware for switching back.

  1. Connect Talko using a USB data cable.
  2. Hold BOOTSEL, press and release RESET, then release BOOTSEL after a second or two.
  3. Copy the UF2 to the RPI-RP2 drive. Leave USB connected until the copy finishes and Talko restarts.
  4. Check the spoken and displayed 1 point 0 version. To switch back, repeat these steps using the standard Talko 2 UF2.

For board access and the standard firmware download, see the Talko 2 user manual.

Startup and idle display

At startup, the front display shows 1, the decimal point, then 0. The voice says « un point zéro ». The rear LED flashes red once, then blue once.

After five idle minutes, a moving display segment replaces the bank or FX page. Turn a knob or the encoder, press a panel button or send a gate to wake it. Active audio or a held ERROR button keeps the display awake.

Source and attribution

The MEA8000 engine is adapted from Antoine Miné’s MEA8000 emulation in MAME, released under BSD-3-Clause. The Talko adaptation adds RP2040 audio, panel controls, stored banks, live pitch/speed/formant controls and the Formant synth.

The historical speech data come from the Arno Jacobs collection, Techni-Musique vocabulary, Polaxis Emy/Mea tables and the Citroën archive. See the source credits and licensing notes.
